What is K&A?

K&A helps trucking and dispatch teams organize MC verification, carrier discovery, qualification and follow-up workflows — turning hours of manual FMCSA lookups into minutes of automated, filtered results.

Our Mission

To give small and mid-size dispatch operations the same speed and clarity that large carrier networks already have — without the enterprise price tag.

Features

  • Live MC range scanning against FMCSA SAFER
  • Automatic qualification filtering
  • Saved carriers & call notes
  • Smart pickup line generator
  • Scan history & reports

Why K&A?

Built for dispatchers, by people who understand the workflow — every feature exists because it solved a real bottleneck in a real day-to-day operation.
